Behind the Resin Studio

The Soul of Mankashay

✦ ✤ ✦

"Art that lasts forever" is more than our tagline — it is our promise to encapsulate the fragile beauty of life in permanent, optical-grade crystal resin.

Where Passion Meets Permanence

From Withered Petals to Everlasting Heirlooms

✦ ✤ ✦

Mankashay was born from a simple heartache: watching meaningful flowers — wedding garlands, graduation bouquets, and flowers handed over during quiet reunions — wilt, dry out, and turn into dust.

We asked ourselves: What if we could pause time at the exact moment when the petal is most vibrant?

Through months of chemical experimentation, trial pours, and botanical drying methods, Mankashay emerged as an artisan studio dedicated to botanical jewelry, flower preservation, and bespoke functional art. Today, we craft for clients in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Quetta, Peshawar, and across rural Pakistan.

Our Methodology

The Alchemy of Slow Craft

✦ ✤ ✦

Every Mankashay piece takes between 5 to 14 days from fresh bloom to final mirror shine.

1

Botanical Foraging

We source local wild roses, miniature forget-me-nots, and jasmine blossoms, selecting only pristine petals at peak bloom.

2

Micro-Desiccant Drying

Flowers are buried in ultra-fine silica sand for 72 hours, extracting every trace of water while preserving 100% of the natural petal dye.

3

Layered Epoxy Pouring

We mix 2:1 ratio UV-stabilized crystal resin in temperature-controlled stages, allowing each layer to cure without trapped micro-bubbles.

4

7-Stage Hand Sanding

Each cured piece is wet-sanded through 400 to 12,000 grit micro-mesh abrasives to eliminate rough edges and seam lines.

5

Buffing & Mounting

A final polish with optical compound produces a glass-like shine. Fitted with hypoallergenic stainless steel or 18k gold-plated findings.

★

Signature Packaging

Tucked into eco-friendly keepsake boxes with personalized botanical tags, ready to gift or treasure forever.
