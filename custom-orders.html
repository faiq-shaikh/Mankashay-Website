How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

✦ ✤ ✦

We receive flowers from brides and customers across Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and every corner of Pakistan.

1

Send Flowers or Idea

Fill out the form below or slide into our Instagram DMs. Pack your flowers using our easy shipping instructions, or share your custom design ideas with us.

2

We Craft with Care

We gently extract moisture using silica crystals to lock in true petal color, then cast layer-by-layer in crystal-clear, non-yellowing epoxy resin.
